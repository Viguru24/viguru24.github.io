Cosmo Symphony

A hardware-accelerated multi-media workspace and AI creative studio for Windows. Orchestrate dozens of 4K/8K videos and RAW photos concurrently, upscale locally on your GPU, design realistic hardwood museum frames, and sync files from Android over local Wi-Fi with zero cloud accounts.

Core Architecture & Capabilities

🦀

Rust & Tauri v2 Native Engine

Ultra-lightweight desktop runtime combining native Rust concurrency with a hardware-accelerated React 18 interface and AV1 Solo Mode playback.

🔍

Local 4K/8K AI Upscaling

Enhance photos and video frames directly on your own GPU—positioned as a sovereign, subscription-free alternative to Topaz Video AI and Adobe creative suites.

🖼️

Hardwood Museum Framing Studio

Create realistic gallery mattes, wood-grain frames, portrait blur effects, and custom stickers without leaving your local workspace.

📡

CosmoShare Local Wi-Fi Sync

Pair the CosmoShare Android companion app over local Wi-Fi for instant, zero-cloud phone-to-PC photo and video transfers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the official Cosmo Symphony source repository?

Cosmo Symphony is developed by Louis De Souza (Viguru24) and hosted openly on GitHub at https://github.com/Viguru24/Video under the GNU GPL v3.0 License.

Is Cosmo Symphony a free Topaz Video AI alternative on Windows?

Yes. Cosmo Symphony is 100% free and open-source (GPL-3.0), performing local GPU upscaling, framing, and multi-video playback completely offline with zero subscriptions or telemetry.

How do I transfer photos from my phone to Cosmo Symphony?

Install the CosmoShare Companion Android APK (v1.3.7) from the Viguru24/Video releases page to transfer media over your local Wi-Fi network directly to Windows.
