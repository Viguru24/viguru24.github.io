Core Architecture & Capabilities

🔒

Zero Cloud Audio Retention

All speech recognition runs locally on your CPU/GPU via OpenAI Whisper models—making it private for coding, notes, and sensitive workflows.

⚡

Global Push-to-Talk Hotkeys

Hold a system-wide shortcut to dictate directly into any active desktop text field across Windows and macOS.

🍎

Native .NET 8 & Metal Performance
