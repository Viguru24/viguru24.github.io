Kin-Tracker

A privacy-first, open-source family safety radar and real-time GPS transit network for Android. Stay connected with arrival alerts and geofences without handing your family's movement history to commercial data brokers.

Core Architecture & Capabilities

🛡️

Zero Data Brokers

Built explicitly as a privacy-respecting Life360 alternative—no ad tracking, no selling location histories, and transparent MIT-licensed source code.

🔋

Adaptive Motion & Battery Optimization

Combines low-power motion sensors with dynamic GPS intervals so background tracking runs all day without draining battery.

🚨

Geofencing & Arrival Alerts

Configure instant arrival/departure notifications for home, school, or work plus quick emergency broadcasts.

🗺️

OpenStreetMap + Material 3

Clean Material 3 Jetpack Compose interface powered by open OpenStreetMap (`osmdroid`) tiles.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is Kin-Tracker different from Life360?

Kin-Tracker is free, open-source (MIT), and sells zero family location data, replacing proprietary tracking tiers with self-sovereign control.

Which Android versions are supported?

Kin-Tracker supports Android 8.0+ via the downloadable APK at https://github.com/Viguru24/Kin-Tracker/releases/latest.
